What your site is costing you — A-1 Mechanical & Electric —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a homeowner with an AC problem, or a business that needs an electrician, would. The site is full, the request forms ask for a day and time, and the pages are light. The leaks are in a few links and numbers that don't line up.

Checked October 16, 2026

What I found

01

A “Service” link on every page points to a test copy of your site.

On all 12 pages I saved, the “Service” link in the footer quick-links goes to bxbhvaclayout11.kinsta.cloud/request-service/ instead of a-1mechanical.com. That address is live: it loads a request-service form, and its homepage title is “Company Name”. It is marked so Google won't list it. I didn't submit it, so I can't say where a message sent there would go.

02

Your business markup is an unfilled template.

The data behind your pages that tells search tools who you are reads: business name “Company Name”, phone “+1-”, address only “United States”, open every day 00:00 to 00:00, and 15 service-area cities that all say “PLACEHOLDER”. 11 of the 12 pages I saved carry it (the Specials page doesn't). Your real name, phone and address are on the page, but this data says otherwise.

03

Phone numbers don't line up.

The offer images on your /residential/ page and the page description for /residential/air-ducts/ tell people to call 702.363.6222. Your header and the other pages I saved use 702.848.3244. Separately, in the page code as delivered, a call link on every page I checked is just “tel:+1” with no number. A ServiceTitan call-tracking script is on the page and may fill it in when it runs in a browser. I didn't test that. One offer image's description also reads “discount of up to 0 off”.

04

The page loads light but the main content is still slow.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 2.2 seconds and its largest part at 6.2 seconds, even though the page is only about 0.9 MB.

05

My accessibility check found a lot of hard-to-read text.

It flagged 38 spots where text is hard to read against its background, for example white text on orange that measured 2.5 and 2.7 against a minimum of 3 to 4.5, including the main headline. It also flagged two unlabeled boxes in the savings calculator.

What's working

Titles and descriptions on all 12 pages I saved. Service, estimate and contact forms that ask for a preferred day and time. Your phone (702.848.3244), license numbers and address on the page, plus Yelp, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube links. In my search for Carrier dealers, you were named.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether your request forms reach you, and whether the call-tracking script fills in the empty call links. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks the WordPress admin area. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Gap
    Business markup is unfilled

    The homepage markup is typed as an HVAC business but its name is “Company Name”, its phone is “+1-”, its address is only “United States”, and its 15 service-area cities all read “PLACEHOLDER”. 11 of the 12 pages I saved carry it.

  • Gap
    A second phone number

    702.363.6222 appears on /residential/ (offer images) and /residential/air-ducts/ (page description). The number everywhere else is 702.848.3244.

  • Gap
    Empty call links

    A call link on every page I checked is “tel:+1”. A call-tracking script may fill it in, so this is unverified.

  • Gap
    A link to a test site

    Every page I saved has a “Service” link to bxbhvaclayout11.kinsta.cloud/request-service/. That address loads and is marked noindex.

  • Note
    Opening hours read as round the clock

    The markup lists every day as open 00:00 to 00:00, which reads as around the clock. Your site also advertises 24/7 emergency service.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
